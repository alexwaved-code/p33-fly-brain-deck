# How the male fruit fly's brain map is advancing neuroscience

A self-guided web deck by Team P3-3 for IT 1020SEF (17 November 2026). It runs in a browser on a laptop or a phone.

**Open it:** https://alexwaved-code.github.io/p33-fly-brain-deck/

## How to use it

- Tap **Next** and **Back** at the bottom, or tap an empty part of the slide. On a keyboard, use **→** and **←**.
- Drag a 3D model to turn it.
- Tap a source chip at the bottom left of a slide to see the quotation and its APA reference.
- On slide 12, tap **Next** once to open the game, then put the hand over the fly and tap.
- On a phone, hold it sideways.

## Run it locally

The deck is plain static files. From this folder:

```
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000.

## Sources

The full reference list in APA format is on the last slide. The neuron shapes and synapse counts come from the MaleCNS v1.0 dataset (Janelia Research Campus, https://male-cns.janelia.org), shared under CC BY 4.0. The game on slide 12 is the team's own small model of ten cells. It is not a recording, and it is not a Janelia product.
